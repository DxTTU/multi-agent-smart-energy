# Energy Policy Knowledge Base

If an appliance is idle for a long period and the action is explicitly approved, it can be switched off to reduce standby consumption.

High-risk appliances such as medical equipment, refrigerators, and safety-critical systems should not be automatically switched off.

During high-tariff periods, the optimizer may recommend shifting flexible loads such as washing machines or water heaters to a lower-tariff period.

A recommendation is preferred when confidence is low. Automatic control should be limited to pre-approved, low-risk appliances.

Every action should be logged with the reason, estimated saving, confidence, and whether it was automatic or recommended.
